## Rules checked (Dubai, English, rules/ only)

- dewa_electricity-installations-regulations_v2022-09.pdf#4.6.9 (Electric Motors and Starters; the body heading is 4.6.9, the table of contents calls it 4.6.10): control of motors shall comply with BS EN 60204 if within its scope; motor control circuits shall prevent unsafe automatic restart; starters shall have thermal overload relays. These are requirements on the motor control installation. A replacement contactor must not break them, but the rule sets no product approval for the contactor itself.
- dewa_electricity-installations-regulations_v2022-09.pdf#Appendix-1 (Standards): lists BS EN 60947 (low-voltage switchgear and controlgear), the family that includes contactors (part 4-1). Section 4.5 refers to this appendix for switchgear and control gear.
