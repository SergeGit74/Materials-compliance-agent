## Rules checked (Dubai, English, rules/ only)

- dewa_electricity-installations-regulations_v2022-09.pdf#4.4 (Cable trays and supports): steel cable trays, accessories and supports shall normally be hot dip galvanized or PVC coated and be perforated or ladder; adequate strength and rigidity; upstands on both sides; fittings of the same quality; earth bonding between sections. I read this in the PDF text. A hot dip galvanised perforated steel tray fits the finish and style wording. Strength and upstands cannot be shown from the listing.
- dewa_electricity-installations-regulations_v2022-09.pdf#4.3.2 (Trunking) also says small insulated cables shall not be installed in perforated trunking or trays. This is a use restriction for the installer, relevant to what the tray will carry (Equipment_Served is "General stock").
