## Rules checked (Dubai, English, rules/ only)

- dewa_electricity-installations-regulations_v2022-09.pdf#4.6.9 (Electric Motors and Starters): every motor above 0.37 kW shall have control equipment with overload protection; starters shall have thermal overload relays; motors shall be protected against short circuit, earth leakage and loss of phase. This pump motor is 2.2 kW, above 0.37 kW. The rule applies to the motor circuit and starter, not to the pump as a product; I cite it as context, and no pump document shows how the motor is protected.
- dewa_electricity-installations-regulations_v2022-09.pdf#5.4.4: earth leakage sensors or relays with alarm shall be provided for fire pumps, jockey pumps, submersible pumps, sump pumps and similar essential circuits. This too concerns circuit protection, not the pump.
- 4.6.9 also limits 3-phase motors to 110 kW without DEWA approval; 2.2 kW is far below that.
