The first run could not open any supplier page, so it held NEEDS_DOCUMENTS. A later fetch opened the Schneider Electric product datasheet for LV431630 (ComPacT NSX250F, 3 poles, TM-D trip unit, 250 A). A copy is saved at results/evidence/INV-0001_LV431630_datasheet.pdf.
Source: https://www.se.com/il/en/product/download-pdf/LV431630?filename=Schneider+Electric_ComPact-NSX_LV431630.pdf

What the datasheet states (text extracted from the PDF; the table layout is scrambled, so each value was matched to its line):
- Range ComPacT NSX100...250, product NSX250F, 3 poles, 250 A at 40 C, rated operational voltage 690 V AC.
- 36 kA Icu at 380/415 V AC 50/60 Hz conforming to IEC 60947-2 (performance level F, 36 kA, 415 V AC). Ics 36 kA at 380/415 V.
- Suitability for isolation: yes, conforming to EN/IEC 60947-2. Standards: EN/IEC 60947. Trip unit TM-D thermal-magnetic.
- Status: discontinued on 15 June 2023. The Schneider page names C25F3TM250 (NSX250F, 36 kA at 415 VAC, TMD 250 A, 3 poles) as the replacement; that page was also opened and gives the same poles, current and breaking capacity but does not list standards.

Code comparison of the row specs (3P, 250 A, 36 kA, 415 V) with the datasheet: FIT, with MATCH on A, kA and V.

Rule applied: dewa_electricity-installations-regulations_v2022-09.pdf#4.5.1 says the switch gear, equipment and accessories shall generally comply with the standards specified in Appendix 1, as applicable (BS EN 60670, BS EN 60898, BS EN 60947, BS EN 61439, IEC 61439). Appendix 1 lists BS EN 60947. I re-read both in the PDF text.

Why ALLOWED: a cited rule in rules/ and an opened supplier document that states conformity to the standard family the rule names, with the row's four numeric specs matching.

Limits that remain:
- The datasheet says EN/IEC 60947, not the BS EN adoption by name. EN 60947-2 and BS EN 60947-2 carry the same technical content; a person should confirm that is acceptable.
- LV431630 was discontinued before the last purchase (2024-03-12), so the stock may be the replacement C25F3TM250, whose own page lists no standards; the ALLOWED covers the NSX250F family datasheet (the analogue), not a confirmed reference for the stock on hand.
- 'Shall generally comply' is not a type-test certificate or declaration of conformity for the stock itself. That would still be good to obtain.
- The fault level at MDB-01 is unknown; 36 kA must cover it. That is a design check outside rules/.
- No DEWA approval list for MCCBs is in rules/.
- human_decision stays pending. This is a recommendation for a person, not an approval.
