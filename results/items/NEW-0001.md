## Final verdict

Notes: Dubai Municipality Code of Construction Safety Practice (Scaffolding, Mobile Tower Scaffolds) applies. It says the tower height must not exceed 4 times the smallest base width, otherwise outriggers must widen the base. At 0.75 m width the limit is about 3 m, so a 6 m tower needs outriggers or stabilisers, or tying/special design. Chemey (maker page, read) lists stabilisers as standard only above 6 m working height. Unitech (maker page, read) lists a 0.75 m custom width. Neither page shows the stabilised base width for this 6 m configuration, so the rule cannot be confirmed as met. Documents needed: (1) a maker erection/assembly manual or configuration drawing showing the 6 m build with outrigger or stabiliser base width and height-to-width ratio, plus castors with positive brakes, guardrails/toe-boards and internal ladder access (mobile tower rule, 8-11-2); (2) the maker's load rating, since the rule has light/medium/heavy duty classes at 1.2/2.4/3.6 kN/m2 and requires a 4x safety factor; the maker pages state 200 kg/m2 and EN 1004; (3) the contractor's scaffold design approved by the engineering office, if required, and a third-party inspection certificate before use. The excerpts do not name EN 1004 as a requirement; the EN 1004 claims come from the maker pages only. The Alfix PDF is another maker's 1.4 m-wide tower and does not settle this product.
