Notes: Verifier removed side detail(s) it could not support: A competent-person training certificate is required as a site-side document under 8-1/8-10. | The Dubai Municipality construction safety code covers mobile tower scaffolds. Rule 8-11-2 limits tower height to 4 times the smallest base width; taller towers need outriggers. A 0.75 m platform width gives only about 3 m, so a 6 m tower needs outriggers or stabilisers. The opened Chemey page lists the 0.75 x 1.8 m ladder-frame tower, EN 1004 and 200 kg/m2, but says stabilizers are standard only above 6 m working height. It does not state the base width with stabilisers. Needed from the maker: a datasheet or erection manual for the 6 m configuration showing stabiliser or outrigger base width (height/width ratio), castors with positive brakes rated for the safe working load, guard rails and toe-boards, and an access ladder. Also needed: the safe working load against the light/medium/heavy duty classes (8-5), with a 4x safety factor. Platform width 0.75 m meets the 60 cm minimum for persons only; it is under the 80 cm minimum when materials are carried. Site-side documents: the scaffold design approved by the engineering office (general requirement 4), third-party inspection certificate and inspection tags (8-1, 8-10). The Unitech page cites EN1004 but lists 0.85 m or 1.45 m widths (custom 0.75 m), so it is weaker evidence.
