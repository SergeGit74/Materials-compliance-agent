Rule 2.15.1.2 requires the damper to be designed and tested per Section 7, and 7.1.33 / 6.1.4 name UL 555, EN 1366-2 and EN 13501-3. Rule 7.1.1 requires Civil Defence Material Approval listing and registration. Rule 2.15.4.1 sets the heat-actuating device temperature: about 27.8 °C above duct temperature and not below 71 °C. The only opened supplier document (SAFID Belimo catalogue) covers the BF24 actuator, not the complete 400x300 mm damper.
