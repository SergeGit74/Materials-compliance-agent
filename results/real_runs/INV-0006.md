## Verification notes

The core claims are supported. The excerpts back 5.4.2 (BS EN 61009), 9.3.2 (30 mA for 13A socket and portable equipment circuits) and 1.6 (Appendix-1 or IEC conformity deemed to satisfy). The opened Legrand datasheet states 1P+N (2 poles), 32 A in the In range, 30 mA sensitivity, Icn 6000 A to EN/IEC 61009-1, and types AC or F, with F also type A. Sections 1.7 and 1.12 are neither cited nor provided in the excerpts, so the statements about prior approval and DEWA inspection are unsupported. This is a side detail.
