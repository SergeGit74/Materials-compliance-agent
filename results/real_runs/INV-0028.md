## Verification notes

The Diehl HYDRUS 2.0 page (opened and read) matches the product: DN 50 brass, ultrasonic, Q3 25 m3/h, MID approval. The Dubai rules do not accept it on specs alone. Building Code G.9.1 and the Green Building rule 602.01 ask for a DEWA main meter and sub-meters, and say meters should be approved by DEWA and comply with DEWA specifications. The Green Building rule also asks for remote data access and data logging. K.9.5 asks for DEWA-approved meters (for the building as a whole). Sample drawing PEW-STDAMI-005 describes a 2 in meter with an LDPE connection, but the DEWA circulars and specifications themselves are not in the excerpts.
