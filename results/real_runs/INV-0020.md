## Verification notes

The diameter failure is well supported. Table 9.5 (#7-HOSE) requires a 25 mm, 30 m hose for hose reel systems. #9-LOCATION and #1-DEFINITION give a 25 mm outlet valve. 1.3.9 gives a 25 mm bore for Class II hose reels. The product's 19 mm hose is below this, so NOT_ALLOWED stands. The supplier pages do state 19 mm x 30 m reels and EN 671 claims, and Safetag and Sensortech claim LPCB. The 25 mm alternatives named are listed on the opened pages. Two statements have no basis in the cited excerpts: that EN 671-1 is on the code's accepted standards list, and that Civil Defence listing or Material Approval Department approval is required. Neither changes the verdict.
