The core claims are supported. DEWA 4.4 does require HDG perforated or ladder steel tray with upstands on both sides. DEWA 6.3.8 and DBC G.4.11 do require BS EN 61537. The candidate data and opened documents give 300x50 mm HDG tray in 3 m lengths, and none names BS EN 61537. The NEEDS_DOCUMENTS verdict stands. Two side details are unsupported: the DEWA 4.3.2 citation and the remark on the scope of telecom rule G.11.4.8.1.
