## Verification notes

The core claims are supported. DEWA 4.2.1 and DBC G.4.7.1 require stranded copper cables to BS 5467 among other listed standards. The Eland datasheet, A9SWA4185, gives 4 cores, 185 mm2, Class 2 copper, XLPE, SWA, 0.6/1kV, BS 5467 and BASEC approval. DBC Table G.6 includes 185 mm2. The single-core armour ban does not apply. The current-rating correction factors are supported by DEWA 7.1.3. Two side details have no support in the cited excerpts: the BS 6121 brass glands and the DEWA 1.7 prior approval.
